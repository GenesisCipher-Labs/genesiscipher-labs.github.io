# Current release availability

Confirmed by the publisher on September 14, 2026: **HomeSafe and Pact have launched in every country except India.** India is the only country exclusion. This supersedes older U.S.-only and “India and neighbouring countries” launch plans.

Store distribution and product capabilities are separate. HomeSafe routing and data coverage remain limited to the cities and service areas supported by each platform and version. Worldwide distribution does not imply worldwide routing coverage. HomeSafe is not offered in the Indian App Store, and from the 2026-09-17 release its in-product Indian city coverage is withheld as well: the mapped Indian city data, currencies and tax features stay bundled and retained, but routing, search, place reads, Help directories and the companion surfaces expose the supported cities. HomeSafe iOS 2.1.6 expands its in-product catalog to eleven US service areas plus Toronto, Vancouver, Montréal, Barcelona, Amsterdam, London, Berlin, Rome and Paris. Capabilities vary by market and platform; this is not a claim of complete municipal coverage or verified local incident feeds. That withholding is a product decision recorded in `MarketAvailabilityPolicy.enabledRegions`, separate from the storefront exclusion above, and it is reversible without rebuilding the retained data.

The public pages deliberately state availability in general terms ("available in most countries") and name no excluded country; this internal record is the precise version. This record reflects the publisher’s confirmation, not an independent audit of every store territory. It does not change App Store Connect or Google Play settings. Earlier dated research, measurements and launch plans are historical; use this record for current availability wording.
