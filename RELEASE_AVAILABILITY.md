# Current release availability

Confirmed by the publisher on September 14, 2026: **HomeSafe and Pact have launched in every country except India.** India is the only country exclusion. This supersedes older U.S.-only and “India and neighbouring countries” launch plans.

Store distribution and product capabilities are separate. HomeSafe routing and data coverage remain limited to the cities and service areas supported by each platform and version. Worldwide distribution does not imply worldwide routing coverage. HomeSafe is not offered in the Indian App Store, but its in-product Indian city coverage is enabled again (restored 2026-09-27, after being withheld from the 2026-09-17 release): Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad are supported for people who downloaded HomeSafe through another country's App Store, and travelling to India does not disable that coverage. The next in-product release contains 42 bounded service areas: the five Indian markets, eleven US service areas, and Toronto, Vancouver, Montréal, Barcelona, Amsterdam, London, Berlin, Rome, Paris, Buenos Aires, São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Perth, Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide, Brisbane, Shanghai, Beijing, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Jakarta, Istanbul, Cairo, Milan and Cape Town. The expansion source and capability review is recorded in `docs/MarketExpansion-2026-10-01.md`. Capabilities vary by market and platform; this is not a claim of complete municipal coverage or verified local incident feeds. In-product coverage is a product decision recorded in `MarketAvailabilityPolicy.enabledRegions`, separate from the storefront exclusion above.

Shanghai and Beijing retain dated place/street directories and reviewed emergency actions. Their in-app routing capability is withheld after native provider failures from the India-hosted test environment, including the documented China-language/location diagnostic. Mainland-China physical-device provider and endpoint validation remains required; this is not a claim that Apple Maps lacks directions throughout China.

The public pages deliberately state availability in general terms ("available in most countries") and name no excluded country; this internal record is the precise version. This record reflects the publisher’s confirmation, not an independent audit of every store territory. It does not change App Store Connect or Google Play settings. Earlier dated research, measurements and launch plans are historical; use this record for current availability wording.
