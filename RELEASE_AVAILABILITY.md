# Current release availability

Confirmed by the publisher on September 14, 2026: **HomeSafe and Pact have launched in every country except India.** India is the only country exclusion. This supersedes older U.S.-only and “India and neighbouring countries” launch plans.

Store distribution and product capabilities are separate. HomeSafe routing and data coverage remain limited to the cities and service areas supported by each platform and version. Worldwide distribution does not imply worldwide routing coverage. HomeSafe supports its mapped Indian cities for people who downloaded it through another country’s App Store. It is not offered in the Indian App Store; travelling to India does not disable supported Indian city coverage. Existing Indian city data, currencies, tax features and client support are not removed by a storefront exclusion.

This record reflects the publisher’s confirmation, not an independent audit of every store territory. It does not change App Store Connect or Google Play settings. Earlier dated research, measurements and launch plans are historical; use this record for current availability wording.
