About HomeSafe

Which cities does HomeSafe cover?

HomeSafe 2.1.6 supports twenty service areas: San Francisco, New York City, Los Angeles, the Peninsula & South Bay, Chicago, Seattle, Boston, Miami, Dallas, Houston, Las Vegas, Toronto, Vancouver, Montréal, Barcelona, Amsterdam, London, Berlin, Rome and Paris. These are bounded product areas, not complete metropolitan coverage. Data and features vary by city; unsupported local incidents and transit information remain unavailable. Outside supported areas the app says it is not in your area yet.

Does HomeSafe call the police or an ambulance for me?

No. HomeSafe never contacts emergency services on your behalf and has no ability to dispatch anyone. Within verified coverage, its Help screen offers numbers you dial yourself, such as 911 in the United States; outside verified coverage it does not guess an emergency number. Messages to trusted contacts are composed for you and sent only when you tap Send.

What does the safety rating actually mean?

It is a 0-100 rating of a route against signals the app can genuinely observe, such as locally supported mapped street-lighting surveys, mapped place results, road type and available weather context. Place results do not establish current opening hours, staffing or crowds. Community reporting is disabled in this build and contributes nothing to the score. Tapping the score shows the full breakdown, free, on every route. It supports your judgement; it is never a guarantee that a route is safe.

Does HomeSafe need an account, and where does my location go?

No HomeSafe account is required. Trips, saved places and history stay on the device. Community reporting is disabled in this build for all markets, so no new report is accepted or sent to CloudKit. Previously submitted records may remain and can still be deleted or moderated; trip updates leave through Messages only after you review them and tap Send.

Is HomeSafe free?

The app is free to download and the safety rating, the full score breakdown, the fare comparison and the Help screen are free for everyone. An optional HomeSafe+ membership adds depth such as route alternatives and the money ledger. Paying never buys a different safety score or faster check-ins.
