## HomeSafe

HomeSafe ranks route options by safety signals from public map data, not ETA alone. It covers Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad in India, and San Francisco, Silicon Valley, Los Angeles and New York City in the United States.

HomeSafe is available on iOS. The Android app is in development — coming soon.

## Pact

Pact turns a brand-deal email into a proper tax invoice, tracks the payment, and drafts the follow-up when it runs late. You state which country you invoice from, and the document follows your rules — your own currency, your own tax by its own name (VAT, USt, TVA or GST), and your bank details labelled the way your country labels them. It was built India-first, so it also goes all the way on Indian GST: the correct CGST/SGST/IGST split, place of supply, TDS, financial-year invoice numbering and a scan-to-pay UPI QR.

Everything runs on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking, and no network connection of Pact's own. Pact runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Free for your first three invoices. Pact Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, and one subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Exporting and backing up your invoices are free and are not part of any subscription.
