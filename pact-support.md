# Pact Support

Email [genesiscipherlabs@gmail.com](mailto:genesiscipherlabs@gmail.com) for help.

Include your platform, app version and a brief description of the problem. Do not email invoices, bank details, tax identifiers or client information.

For purchases, use the store account that made the purchase to manage subscriptions or request a refund. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Before deleting app data or reinstalling, save any records you need using your version’s export or backup controls. Previously exported files are managed separately.
