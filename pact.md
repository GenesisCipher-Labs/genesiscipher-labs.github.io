# Pact — Brand Deal Invoices

Pact turns a brand's collaboration email into a professional, GST-compliant
invoice — in seconds, entirely on your device. Built for Indian creators and
freelancers who are tired of chasing payments and wrestling with spreadsheets.

Pact runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac — one subscription covers all three.

## How it works

- Paste or share the brand's email.
- Pact reads the deal — brand, amount, deliverables, and payment terms.
- Review, then generate a clean invoice with correct GST (CGST/SGST/IGST) and
  financial-year-continuous numbering.
- Track the payment and get reminders until you're paid.
- Send a polished follow-up when a payment runs late.

## Private by design

Pact reads your emails entirely on your device — using Apple Intelligence where
available, or its built-in parser otherwise. Your deals and earnings never leave
your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Getting your data out is free and always will be: export every invoice PDF plus
a CSV ledger and a GST/TDS summary whenever you like, and nominate a folder for
a dated daily backup.

## Pricing

Free for life: your first 3 invoices. Trying a sample never spends one.

**Pact Pro** adds unlimited invoices; your own logo and no "Made with Pact" line;
invoices for brands outside India; and follow-ups rewritten in your voice on
devices with Apple Intelligence. Pact Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed
weekly, monthly, 3-monthly or 6-monthly — prices are shown in the app, and one
subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Manage or cancel any time in
Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Full billing terms are in the
[Terms of Use](./terms/).

Exporting your invoices and backing them up to a folder are **free** and are not
part of any subscription.
